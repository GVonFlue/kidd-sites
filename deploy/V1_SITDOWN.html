How to run this — 90 minutes

1. Before he arrives (10 min). Open kidd-sites.vercel.app/?brand=agent and ?brand=cornerstone in two tabs, plus this checklist in a third. Have your phone out — half the review has to happen on a phone, because that is where his buyers are.
2. Do not start with the website (5 min). Start with the ASK LIST below and get every credential and fact first. If you leave it to the end you will run out of time, and those are the only things that actually block launch. The design opinions are not blocking; they are a punch list.
3. Walk it in his order, not ours (45 min). Screen-share and scroll. For each section press KEEP or CHANGE. Only type when he says something specific — the note field appears when you press CHANGE. Do not debate anything in the room; capture it and move on. Arguing costs more meeting time than any single change is worth.
4. Close with the three decisions (10 min). Launch date, which email leads go to, and whether he wants the buyer guide going out automatically on day one.
5. Press "Copy the list" and send it to me. It comes out as a clean punch list. Nothing is saved if you refresh this page, so copy it before you close the tab.
